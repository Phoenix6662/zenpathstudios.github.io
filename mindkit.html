MindKit

Mental wellness tools that actually help in real life

MindKit brings together journaling, mood tracking, grounding exercises, affirmations, and practical self-help tools in one place. Whether you're feeling overwhelmed, stressed, stuck, or just trying to get through the day, MindKit gives you something useful to reach for.

Download on Google Play

What MindKit is for

MindKit was built for people who want support without digging through ten different apps.

Need to clear your head? Open the journal.

Trying to understand a rough day? Check in with your mood tracker.

Feeling overwhelmed? Use a grounding exercise.

Everything is designed to be simple, practical, and easy to use when you need it most.

Features

  • Journal thoughts, experiences, and reflections
  • Track moods and wellness patterns over time
  • Access grounding exercises and coping tools
  • Build positive habits with affirmations and reminders
  • Explore a collection of mental wellness resources in one place
