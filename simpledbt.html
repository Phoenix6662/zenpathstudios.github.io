What SimpleDBT is for

SimpleDBT is for the moments when you know you need a coping skill, but you do not want to dig through a workbook or decode clinical language.

It helps you find practical DBT skills quickly, understand what they are for, and use them in real life when emotions feel intense.

Features

  • Plain-language DBT skill explanations
  • Quick access when emotions feel intense
  • Tools for distress tolerance, emotion regulation, mindfulness, and interpersonal moments
  • Built for real-life coping, not therapy homework
