Momentum

A softer way to keep yourself moving

Momentum is for the days when you have things to do, but starting feels heavier than it should. It gives you a calm place to name what matters, choose a next step, and build a little rhythm without turning life into a productivity contest.

Explore Momentum

What Momentum is for

Momentum is for people who do better with gentle structure than pressure. It helps you organize routines, goals, and small next steps so your plans feel visible instead of floating around in your head.

When you feel stuck or scattered, Momentum is meant to help you start small, notice progress, and come back to what you were trying to do without shame.

Features

  • Simple routine and goal planning
  • Small-step support for hard-to-start tasks
  • Progress views that focus on consistency, not perfection
  • A calm place to regroup when your plans feel scattered
  • Support for building trust with yourself over time
