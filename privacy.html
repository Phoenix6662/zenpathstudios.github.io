Privacy Policy

Privacy information for ZenPath Studios

This Privacy Policy explains how ZenPath Studios collects, uses, and shares information across our website, contact forms, apps, and related services.

Overview

ZenPath Studios ("ZenPath," "we," "us," or "our") creates mental wellness tools, apps, videos, books, worksheets, and related self-help resources. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our website, contact forms, apps, and related services.

Information You May Collect

We may collect information you choose to provide directly, such as your name, email address, and the contents of any message you send through our contact form or other support channels.

We may also collect limited technical or usage information needed to operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website, apps, and services.

How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to questions and support requests, provide and improve our website and apps, maintain security and reliability, and communicate with you about the product or request you contacted us about.

Website and App Services

ZenPath Studios offers multiple products and resources. Each ZenPath app may have its own separate account system, settings, and data practices. Managing or deleting data for one app does not automatically apply to other ZenPath apps.

As ZenPath Studios grows, individual apps or services may also include product-specific privacy details, account deletion instructions, or platform-specific disclosures.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate our website, contact forms, apps, hosting, support systems, or related technical infrastructure.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, responding to inquiries, maintaining records, and meeting legal or operational needs.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to any legal or operational limitations.

If your request relates to a specific app, please identify that app in your message so we can direct your request appropriately.

Children's Privacy

ZenPath Studios' website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at zenpathstudios@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the date below.
