What SimpleCBT is for

SimpleCBT is designed for people who want more clarity around their thoughts, beliefs, and reactions. It offers grounded tools for slowing down, reframing, and building insight without sounding robotic or detached.

The overall feel is warm, usable, and supportive, especially for users who need structure without losing emotional nuance.

Features

  • Thought reframing and self-awareness exercises
  • Grounding tools for spirals and anxious loops
  • Gentle prompts for insight and pattern recognition
  • Practical support users can revisit often
