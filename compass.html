What Compass is for

Compass is a personal planning system designed for people balancing many roles at once.

Compass gives you one place for your tasks, goals, projects, routines, and plans. Instead of bouncing between apps, sticky notes, and mental reminders, you can keep everything together and see what needs your attention next.

Compass was built for people who have a lot going on.

Work. Home. School. Health. Side projects. Random ideas at 11 PM.

Instead of scattering everything across different apps and notebooks, Compass gives you one place to keep track of it all.

Compass was created by a psychologist who got tired of using five different apps to keep track of life.

Tasks, goals, projects, routines, events, and reminders all live in one place so you can spend less time organizing and more time doing.

The goal isn't to become a productivity machine. The goal is knowing what's next and not feeling like you're forgetting something important.

Features

  • Daily Focus dashboard centered around today
  • Life “Hats” for organizing different roles and responsibilities
  • Tasks, goals, projects, calendar events, and repeating routines
  • Streaks and progress views that make momentum easier to see
  • Designed to reduce overwhelm and create calm clarity
